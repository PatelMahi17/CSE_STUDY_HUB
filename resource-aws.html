AWS Certified Solutions Architect Study Guide

Comprehensive study guide and resources for preparing for the AWS Certified Solutions Architect - Associate exam (or higher).

Why AWS Certification?

Amazon Web Services (AWS) is the leading cloud platform, offering a vast array of services. The AWS Solutions Architect - Associate certification validates your ability to design and implement scalable, highly available, and fault-tolerant systems on AWS. This guide provides resources to help you prepare.

Top Online Courses for Certification Prep

  • Ultimate AWS Certified Solutions Architect - Associate 2025 (Udemy by Stephane Maarek): Highly recommended and frequently updated course known for its clear explanations and hands-on labs.
  • AWS Certified Solutions Architect - Associate (A Cloud Guru): Another popular platform offering comprehensive video courses, labs, and practice exams.
  • AWS Skill Builder: Official training from AWS, offering free digital training and paid learning paths specifically designed for certifications. Explore AWS Skill Builder

Essential Study Materials & Practice Exams

  • Official AWS Certified Solutions Architect - Associate Study Guide: Published by Sybex, it's a detailed guide aligned with the exam objectives.
  • Jon Bonso's Practice Exams (Tutorials Dojo): Regarded as some of the best practice exams available, offering detailed explanations for each answer.
  • AWS Whitepapers: Key whitepapers like "Architecting for the Cloud: AWS Best Practices" and "AWS Well-Architected Framework" are crucial for understanding AWS philosophy and common design patterns. Access AWS Whitepapers

Hands-On Practice & Key Services

  • AWS Free Tier: Utilize the AWS Free Tier to practice with services like EC2, S3, RDS, Lambda, VPC, IAM without incurring significant costs.
  • Key Services to Focus On:
    • Compute: EC2, Lambda, ECS, EKS
    • Storage: S3, EBS, EFS, Glacier, Storage Gateway
    • Databases: RDS, DynamoDB, Aurora, Redshift
    • Networking: VPC, Route 53, Load Balancers, CloudFront
    • Security: IAM, KMS, Shield, WAF, Security Groups, NACLs
    • Management & Monitoring: CloudWatch, CloudTrail, CloudFormation, Trusted Advisor
  • **Build a Simple Project:** Try deploying a basic web application on EC2, connecting it to an RDS database, and putting it behind a Load Balancer to understand service integration.
